Evaluating Technical Analysis Tools Within Learning Platforms

How do you assess whether an app’s charting tools are actually teaching you something? Here’s a framework to evaluate any educational trading platform.

The 5-Point Evaluation Checklist

Use this checklist when comparing apps:

  1. Context Awareness: Does the AI know which coin and timeframe you’re viewing? Generic answers are less useful than context-specific explanations.
  2. Explanation Quality: Does the tool explain why a pattern matters, or just label it? Look for “indicator conflict detection” — a feature that flags when RSI and MACD tell different stories.
  3. Data Quantification: Are concepts like volatility and momentum quantified (e.g., risk/reward ratios) or just described in vague terms?
  4. Privacy Architecture: Is AI processing on-device or in the cloud? On-device processing ensures your data isn’t sent to external servers.
  5. Pricing Transparency: Is there a free trial? Chartscope offers a 3-day free trial at €4.99/month, with no hidden costs for premium features.

Understanding the Underlying Indicators

Before you can evaluate an app’s explanations, you need baseline knowledge of key indicators. Relative Strength Index (RSI) measures the magnitude of recent price changes to evaluate overbought or oversold conditions. Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) shows the relationship between two moving averages of a security’s price. An educational app should explain these in plain language, not just display them as lines on a chart.
