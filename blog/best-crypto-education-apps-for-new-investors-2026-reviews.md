The market for crypto education apps is crowded, and not all of it is quality. Here are the most common mistakes new investors make when selecting a learning platform.

### Pitfall 1: Confusing Signals with Education

The most expensive mistake is paying for a "signals app" thinking it's educational. Signal apps tell you what to buy or sell; education apps teach you how to analyze. The former creates dependency; the latter creates competence. If an app's primary interface is a "BUY" or "SELL" button, it is not an education tool.

### Pitfall 2: Ignoring the Fine Print on Data Collection

Many "free" education apps monetize your data. They track your portfolio, analyze your trading patterns, and sell that information to third parties. Before downloading any app, read its privacy policy. Look specifically for:

- Whether AI processing happens on-device or in the cloud
- Whether the company collects personal identification information
- Whether usage analytics are tracked

### Pitfall 3: Overlooking Platform Compatibility

### Pitfall 4: Skipping the Trial Period

Never pay for a subscription without testing the app first. A 3-day free trial is enough to evaluate whether the teaching style matches your learning preference. If an app doesn't offer a trial, that's a red flag.

### Pitfall 5: Expecting Overnight Mastery
