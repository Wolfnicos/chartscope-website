## Step 2: Choose Your Trading Strategy


<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0 2rem 0;"><img src="https://pub-2df00e47a1bc4a99abcb17ec68f147ce.r2.dev/hive/body/3b86d034-0680-4c1a-84c7-3d4186fa106d.jpg" alt="Step 2: Choose Your Trading Strategy" style="width:100%;max-height:420px;object-fit:cover;border-radius:8px;display:block;"></figure>

Successful trading relies on a defined methodology. Most beginners start with one of these three approaches:
1. **Scalping:** Executing multiple trades throughout the day to profit from small price changes.
2. **Day Trading:** Opening and closing positions within the same 24-hour window to avoid overnight risk.
3. **Swing Trading:** Holding assets for days or weeks to capture larger market moves based on technical indicators.

## Step 3: Risk Management Protocols


<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0 2rem 0;"><img src="https://pub-2df00e47a1bc4a99abcb17ec68f147ce.r2.dev/hive/body/f19a58e9-b865-4ba4-9e9f-26ae43c60a63.png" alt="Step 3: Risk Management Protocols" style="width:100%;max-height:420px;object-fit:cover;border-radius:8px;display:block;"></figure>

Data shows that the most successful traders prioritize capital preservation over high-risk gains. Follow these three rules:
* **The 1% Rule:** Never risk more than 1% of your total portfolio on a single trade.
* **Stop-Loss Orders:** Always set an automated exit point to limit potential losses.
* **Position Sizing:** Adjust your trade size based on the volatility of the specific asset.
