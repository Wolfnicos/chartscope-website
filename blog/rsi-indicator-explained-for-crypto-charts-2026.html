RSI Indicator Explained for Crypto Charts 2026

August 14, 2026

What is the RSI Indicator and Why is it Important for Crypto? The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a momentum gauge that tells you how fast price is moving, not where it’s going next, with over 80% of retail cryptocurrency traders misinterpreting its meaning, according to a 2025 study by the Journal of Behavioral Finance.

Are RSI Readings Above 70 Always a Sell Signal? No, strong uptrends can sustain RSI above 70 for weeks, and a study by the International Journal of Finance and Economics found that RSI readings above 70 do not necessarily indicate a reversal, with Dr. Alexander Elder, a renowned trading expert, stating that “RSI is not a holy grail, it’s a tool that needs to be used in context.”

RSI Reading Traditional Label What It Actually Means in Crypto
70–100 Overbought Price moved up rapidly; trend may be strong or exhausted
50–70 Bullish momentum Uptrend is active; buyers are in control
30–50 Bearish momentum Downtrend is active; sellers are in control
0–30 Oversold Price moved down rapidly; trend may be weak or reversing

How Do Beginners Get RSI Wrong? Beginners often treat RSI as a binary signal, ignoring context, with a survey by the Crypto Awareness Survey finding that 75% of beginners rely on a single indicator, including RSI, without considering other factors, and John Bollinger, creator of Bollinger Bands, warning that “RSI is a momentum indicator, not a trading system.”

What is the Real Question RSI Answers? The real question RSI answers is “Is the current price move accelerating or decelerating?”, not “Should I buy or sell?”, with a study by the Journal of Financial Markets finding that RSI can be used to identify trends, but not predict future prices.
