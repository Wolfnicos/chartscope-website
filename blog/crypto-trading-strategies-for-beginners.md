## 1. Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)


<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0 2rem 0;"><img src="https://pub-2df00e47a1bc4a99abcb17ec68f147ce.r2.dev/hive/body/2dfc86d7-0a4a-4852-8cfb-b9347bc28317.jpg" alt="1. Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)" style="width:100%;max-height:420px;object-fit:cover;border-radius:8px;display:block;"></figure>

DCA is the most recommended strategy for beginners. Instead of trying to "time the market" by investing a lump sum, you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals (e.g., $50 every Monday).

*   **Why it works:** It removes the emotional stress of price fluctuations.
*   **The benefit:** You buy more crypto when prices are low and less when prices are high, effectively lowering your average cost per coin over time.

## 2. Swing Trading


<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0 2rem 0;"><img src="https://pub-2df00e47a1bc4a99abcb17ec68f147ce.r2.dev/hive/body/42d1d0b7-846e-4b45-8217-badc34c6a57e.jpg" alt="2. Swing Trading" style="width:100%;max-height:420px;object-fit:cover;border-radius:8px;display:block;"></figure>

Swing trading involves holding assets for days or weeks to capture "swings" in price. Unlike day trading, which requires constant screen time, swing trading relies on technical analysis to identify trends.

*   **How to execute:** Use indicators like Moving Averages or RSI to spot entry and exit points.

## 3. Essential Risk Management


<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0 2rem 0;"><img src="https://pub-2df00e47a1bc4a99abcb17ec68f147ce.r2.dev/hive/body/059d9de6-f221-46c1-af21-bd7523b7bbba.jpg" alt="3. Essential Risk Management" style="width:100%;max-height:420px;object-fit:cover;border-radius:8px;display:block;"></figure>

No strategy is complete without risk management. Even the best traders lose money; the goal is to ensure those losses don't wipe out your portfolio.

1.  **The 1% Rule:** Never risk more than 1% of your total portfolio on a single trade.
2.  **Use Stop-Loss Orders:** Always set an automatic exit point to limit potential losses if the market moves against you.
3.  **Diversification:** Don't put all your capital into a single "moonshot" coin.

## FAQ

**What is the best crypto trading strategy for a complete beginner?**
Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) is widely considered the best strategy for beginners because it minimizes the impact of market volatility and removes the need for complex technical analysis.

**How much money should I start with in crypto?**
You should only start with an amount you are 100% prepared to lose. Most experts recommend starting small—even $20 to $50—to learn the mechanics of the exchange and the feel of the market.

**Is swing trading better than day trading?**
For beginners, swing trading is generally more manageable than day trading. Day trading requires intense focus and rapid decision-making, whereas swing trading allows more time to analyze charts and manage risk effectively.
